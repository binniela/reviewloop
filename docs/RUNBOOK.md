# When something goes wrong: a guide for the GM

Keep this at the front desk. Open **Operations** (the pulse icon at the bottom of the left rail) first.
A red number on that icon means something needs a person. The list at the top of Operations is the same
list that pages Reviewloop support, so you are both looking at the same thing.

- **Now** (red): guests or you may not be hearing from us. Act within the hour.
- **Today**: look at it before end of day.

Reviewloop support: ______________________ (phone) · ______________________ (email)

---

## Texts aren't going out

You'll see **"Texts are failing to send"** or **"GM alerts are failing"**.

1. **Guests come first.** Open **Issues** and handle open issues the way you did before Reviewloop:
   call the room or send someone. Don't wait for the texts to come back.
2. Tell the desk to keep the Issues page open. It chimes on new issues even when texting is down,
   because guests' texts still arrive.
3. Call Reviewloop support and read them the line under the red alert (for example "Latest error: …").
   Nothing more is needed from you. Texts retry on their own once the problem is fixed, and none are lost.

**"Some texts may not have gone out"** means a text stopped mid-send. Support checks with the
carrier before anything is resent, so a guest is never texted twice. Nothing to do.

**"Texts not delivered"** means the carrier refused a few texts (a landline, or a wrong or blocked number).
Click the guest's name, then call them or catch them at the desk. The system itself is fine.

## Google reviews stopped updating

You'll see **"Google review sync failing"**. If the message mentions `invalid_grant`, `revoked` or `401`:

1. Guests are not affected. Texts, issues and review requests keep working.
2. Call support. The Google account that manages your listing needs to be reconnected, which takes
   five minutes with whoever owns that Google login.
3. Meanwhile, reply to new reviews directly in Google Business Profile if one can't wait.

## Today's reports aren't in

You'll see **"Today's reports aren't in"**. Welcome texts and review requests are timed from the ASI
reports.

1. Export the **In-house** and **Departures** reports from ASI, the same way as every day.
2. Go to **Upload**, choose the file, and upload it. Uploading twice is safe.

## Scheduled work isn't running

You'll see **"Scheduled work isn't running"**. Support has already been paged. Until it's fixed, nobody is
texted when an issue sits untaken, and follow-ups wait. So:

1. Watch the **Issues** page yourself, and take or resolve each new issue promptly.
2. Nothing else. Everything that was waiting goes out once it's running again.

## Reviewloop won't open at all

The page doesn't load, shows an error, or the desktop app shows "Can't reach Reviewloop".

1. Check another website. If nothing loads, it's the hotel's internet.
2. If other sites work, call support. Reviewloop is down and support has been paged.
3. Meanwhile, run the desk the way you did before Reviewloop. Guest texts sent while it's down are kept by
   the carrier and retried, and they appear on the Issues page when it comes back.

## A guest says they never got a text

1. Search for the guest on **Issues**. If they're there, open the conversation and look for **Not delivered**.
2. If they're not there, check that they're in today's ASI report with a mobile number. Texts only go to
   guests who agreed to them, and never between 8 PM and 9 AM.
3. If they replied **STOP**, they won't get texts until they text **START** to the hotel number.
   Nobody at the hotel can override that.
